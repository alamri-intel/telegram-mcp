# Starter pack: research topics and releases

For users following a research field, a technology, or releases of
specific software, products or datasets.

## Extra interview options

- **What matters** (multi-select): new papers or preprints · software
  releases and changelogs · product launches · datasets · conferences and
  deadlines · expert discussion.
- **Depth**: only major releases · anything new · discussion too.
- **Specific items**: projects, labs, authors, products to track by name.

## Seed vocabulary

- Topic terms with synonyms, acronyms and the field's jargon; the names of
  key projects, labs, authors and products, with their handles.
- Release markers: released, launch, "now available", v\d+(\.\d+)+,
  changelog, preprint, arXiv, "paper", DOI patterns.

## Typical noise

Reposts of the same announcement · beginner questions · hype posts with no
link or detail · course and event promotions · old papers resurfacing.

## Suggested rule groups

1. **Named items** — projects, products, labs, authors.
2. **Topic vocabulary** — broader; tune its volume carefully.
3. **Release markers** — version numbers and release words.

## Example criterion

> `new-release` — An announcement or first-hand report of a new release,
> paper or dataset from a tracked project, lab or topic, with a link or
> specific detail. High: major release of a named item. Medium: minor
> releases and relevant new papers. Excludes: reposts, hype without detail,
> questions, promotions, items older than a month.
