# Starter pack: jobs and opportunities

For users looking for jobs, contracts, freelance work, grants or calls for
proposals.

## Extra interview options

- **Roles**: titles, skills and seniority wanted.
- **Terms** (multi-select): full-time · contract · freelance · internship ·
  remote · hybrid · on-site.
- **Location**: countries and cities, time zones, relocation yes/no.
- **Deal-breakers**: industries, company types or conditions to exclude.

## Seed vocabulary

- Role titles and their variants and abbreviations (e.g. "SWE", "Sr.",
  "Lead"), skills and tools, in every language the channels use.
- Posting words: hiring, "we're looking for", vacancy, opening, position,
  apply, "send CV", "DM me" — translated.
- Terms: remote, relocation, visa sponsorship, salary ranges and currencies.

## Typical noise

People advertising their own availability · recruiter cold-calls with no
named company · "we're growing" posts with no listing · courses and
bootcamp ads · roles in the wrong location or seniority.

## Suggested rule groups

1. **Roles and skills.**
2. **Hiring vocabulary**, to catch posts that name the skill but not the title.

## Example criterion

> `matching-role` — A specific open role matching the user's roles and terms,
> posted by someone hiring for it, naming the organization and the role.
> High: an exact match at a preferred organization. Medium: other matches.
> Excludes: self-promotion, recruiter posts with no named company, roles
> requiring relocation (unless accepted), courses and events.
