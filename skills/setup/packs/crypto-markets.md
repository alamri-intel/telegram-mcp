# Starter pack: crypto and markets

For users tracking tokens, projects, exchanges and market-moving news.

## Extra interview options

- **What matters** (multi-select): listings and delistings · launches and
  airdrops · hacks and exploits · rug pulls and scam warnings · regulation ·
  large transfers · partnerships and unlocks.
- **Which assets**: specific tokens or projects · a chain or ecosystem ·
  the whole market.
- **Speed vs certainty**: first rumor · confirmed announcements only.

## Seed vocabulary

- Asset names, tickers with and without `$`, contract addresses, project
  handles, chain names.
- Event words: listing, delisting, launch, airdrop, unlock, hack, exploit,
  drained, rug, "rug pull", paused, halted, SEC, lawsuit, partnership.
- Address formats when useful: `0x[0-9a-fA-F]{40}` and chain-specific ones.

## Typical noise

Price predictions and "to the moon" posts · paid shilling and giveaways ·
referral links · generic market recaps · the same announcement repeated
across channels · bot-generated price tickers.

## Suggested rule groups

1. **Assets and projects** — names, tickers, handles (`word` rules for short
   tickers).
2. **Event vocabulary.**
3. **Addresses** — only for specific contracts or wallets being watched.

## Example criterion

> `asset-event` — An announcement or credible report of a concrete event
> affecting a tracked asset: listing or delisting, hack or exploit, contract
> pause, regulatory action, or a scheduled unlock. High: hack, exploit or
> delisting. Medium: listing, unlock, regulatory news. Excludes: price
> predictions, promotions and giveaways, recaps, reposts of old news.
